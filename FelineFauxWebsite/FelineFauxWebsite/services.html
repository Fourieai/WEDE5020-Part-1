WHAT WE DO

Our Services

Creating awareness, encouraging ethical choices, and supporting the protection of wildlife.

OUR APPROACH

Creating Change Through Awareness

Feline Faux focuses on educating communities about animal welfare and encouraging alternatives to ethical living while reducing carbon footprint. Our work aims to help people understand how their choices can contribute to a more ethical and sustainable future.

01

Education

We provide educational information about animal welfare, the industries that increase carbon footprint and the environmental impact of wildlife exploitation.

Our goal is to help people make informed decisions by understanding where high emissions come from and how they affect wildlife.

02

Awareness Campaigns

We promote awareness campaigns that highlight the challenges faced by vulnerable animals and encourage people to support wildlife protection.

Through digital content and public education, we aim to give wildlife a stronger voice.

03

Ethical Alternatives

We encourage consumers, fashion communities and other polluting industries to explore cruelty-free and sustainable alternatives.

Choosing alternatives can help reduce demand for products that contribute to environmental exploitation.

04

Wildlife Advocacy

We advocate for the protection of vulnerable wildlife and the ecosystems they depend on.

Our message focuses on creating a future where wildlife can survive without unnecessary exploitation.
